Model assumptions

        
Spirit input: Before Divine Spirit preserves the original manual model: add 40 when enabled. Final exported totals use Spirit exactly as entered; the additive buff control is disabled. Unbuffed baseline mode instead calculates selected Divine Spirit, Mark, Living Spirit and Kings contributions once, and shows the resulting total. It never tries to reverse buff multipliers from an already-buffed export.

        
Rake usage: Maintain Rake is the default. Only while Rip is down uses Rake as a bleed fallback for Rend and Tear: cast it only when both Rip and Rake are absent. Rip still takes priority when its CP and energy requirements are met; Shifting Power and terminal Bite keep their existing priorities. Existing Rake is not removed when Rip lands. Lookahead does not reserve a Rake refresh while Rip covers the target, or alongside a planned Rip refresh. Run, Replay, the optimizer, and Bite tradeoffs all use the selected mode; searches keep it fixed. Rend and Tear affects damage only when experimental damage is enabled.

        
Berserk GCD: when Berserk is learned, enabled by default: Berserk at −1s, first attack at 0s, 14s of combat uptime, cooldown ready at 179s. Disabled: Berserk at 0s with the first attack, 15s uptime, cooldown ready at 180s; later uses are also off GCD. If Faerie Fire is enabled, cast it at −2s with learned on-GCD Berserk or −1s with off-GCD / unlearned Berserk. Unlearned Berserk is never cast. Pre-pull casts count in totals; regeneration, attacks, and measured combat time start at 0s.

        
Clearcasting: vanilla WoWSims Classic model: 2 PPM, 10s internal cooldown, 15s buff. Landed cat autos roll 3.33%; landed specials use equipped weapon speed × 2 / 60. The starting build uses its equipped 3.6s weapon; set yours above. Swing timer only changes autoattack frequency. Rake, Shred, Rip, and Bite consume the buff even on avoidance; landed Bite still drains remaining energy. Shifting Power neither benefits from nor consumes Clearcasting.

        
Starting Clearcasting: optional, requires Omen of Clarity. Assumes a fresh proc at pull (t=0): 15s buff remaining and no new Omen roll before t=10s, even after the free cast consumes it. Pre-pull Faerie Fire/Berserk do not shorten it. Shifting Power does not consume it. The starting buff is reported separately from rolled procs; its free cast and energy savings are included. Optimization keeps this starting condition fixed.

        
Natural Shapeshifter: 0/10/20/30% reduction makes Shifting Power cost 684/615.6/547.2/478.8 mana, without integer rounding. Energy gain, cooldown, GCD, and the five-second-rule reset are unchanged.

        
Reflection: retain 0/17/33/50% of Spirit regeneration inside the five-second rule. Outside it, Spirit regeneration is always 100%.

        
Mana buffs: Mana Spring restores 10 mana every 2s from t=2s (25 MP5), or 12.5 (31.25 MP5) with 5/5 Restorative Totems, even inside the five-second rule. Forever talent data checked October 5 still includes this talent. Optional external Mana Tide drops at min(40s, half the fight), restores 290 mana every 3s four times, and repeats every 300s. Judgment of Wisdom assumes full uptime and rolls the selected proc chance for 59 mana on autos, including missed/dodged/parried autos and Windfury attacks. Rake, Shred, Rip, and Bite must land to trigger Judgment; non-melee actions do not trigger it. Mana gains are capped at maximum mana.

        
Windfury: user-confirmed Forever behavior: one immediate extra white attack after the triggering hit, without resetting, advancing, or delaying the next normal swing. No batching delay. Retained WoWSims Classic assumptions: 20% on landed autos and Rake/Shred/Rip/Bite, 1.5s internal cooldown, with the feral restriction removed. Extra attacks can miss/dodge/parry/crit and proc Omen or Judgment; the ICD prevents chaining. No GCD, energy, or CP gain. Damage uses the Forever fork’s temporary AP effect, applied only after triggering damage. Proc chance, ICD, and AP-buff details are separate assumptions, not newly confirmed by the timing report.

        
Shift timing: automatic mode compares cap loss now (including regeneration during Shift's GCD) against later cap loss + energy gain × delay / cooldown. It considers spending first, Clearcasting, known Tea, and gear bonuses; ties favor shifting now. Imminent cooldowns use the actual delay caused by another GCD. Forecasts assume landed attacks and only guaranteed bonus CP, without predicting new random procs. This is a local energy-loss heuristic, not a guarantee of maximum CPM. Manual mode restores the fixed threshold. Shift remains highest priority when the selected rule favors it and mana is available. The optimizer holds the mode fixed and skips threshold search in automatic mode.

        
Shred & Tea: Shred's 4s refresh lookahead counts regeneration during the GCD, reachable off-GCD Tea, and Shifting Power's mana, selected timing mode, cooldown, and full GCD. It checks Rake/Rip together and only pools if Shred would delay a protected refresh compared with waiting. Tea is checked immediately after spending energy as well as between GCDs; the selected ≤10-energy policy still applies.

        
Faerie Fire: lowest-priority filler, only when a full 1s GCD will not delay a known higher-priority action. Forecasts include scheduled energy/mana ticks, cooldowns, bleed and buff timing, and usable Tea. Future random procs are not predicted; any affordable Shred is conservatively protected even while pooling for a refresh.

        
Model note: failed finishers retain combo points and spend their base energy cost unless Clearcasting is active. OOM means the first time Shifting Power is due by policy but its current mana cost is unavailable.
